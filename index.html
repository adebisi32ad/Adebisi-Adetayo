About

I design identities that feel inevitable — as if they were always meant to exist, only waiting to be found.

For the past ten years I've partnered with founders, publishers, and cultural institutions to shape how they look, sound, and feel. My work sits at the intersection of typography, art direction, and restraint.

I believe the best design disappears into the experience — legible, generous, and unmistakably itself. Before going independent I led brand at a studio in New York; today I work with a small handful of clients each year.
